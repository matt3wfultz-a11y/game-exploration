game-exploration

Small, complete games. Each one runs by opening its file — no build step.

  • Reverse dungeon crawler
    Pest Control
    You are the dungeon. Place traps and monsters, then watch an AI hero try to reach your vault. He remembers what killed him and comes back equipped for it.
  • Prototype · adversarial director
    The Warden
    A top-down crawler where you play the hero and the dungeon studies you, building each next room to punish how you played the last one.
node build.js bundles each game into a single self-contained file in dist/.
